    Questions, answered

    Do I need a credit card to start?

    No. The Free plan needs only an email address, and it stays free forever.

    How fast will I be alerted?

    Most teams are alerted within 30 seconds of a failed check. The exact time depends on your plan's check interval.

    Can I switch plans later?

    Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes apply from your next billing date.

    Where do the checks run from?

    From up to 24 regions around the world on the Scale plan. Free and Team plans use a smaller set of regions.

    Is Beacon a real product?

    No. Beacon is a fictional company created for classroom practice. Nothing on this page is real.
